# Holder outreach template (H3 draft)

Draft only — nothing has been sent. Written 17 Nov 2026. Per [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) H3: each of the
213 holders gets their circuit's page link, in Chinese and English, once their circuit has run —
send only after the job has finalized. Replace `{circuit page link}` with the link from
[holders.csv](holders.csv) (column `page_link`), and `{n}` / `{gates}` with the row's beats and
gate count. Start with the non-FAB processors (LoteGate, Covenant, AlephTape, My hero, TAPELIQUID,
Nandverse, GateX), then the biggest FAB holders. One holder at a time; keep the fire small until
it's hot.

**Do not promise votes (H5 asks only after they've seen their own certificate), prices, or dates.**
The page link is the product: their machine, run, with a public record.

## Template (English)

> Hi! We're Overclock — we run TapeOut circuits that are too big for a block, on X Layer.
>
> We ran your circuit (`{processor name}` #`{id}`, `{gates}` gates) for `{n}` beats and posted the
> result on mainnet with a bond anyone could challenge. Here's your circuit's page, with the run,
> every transaction, and the cost next to what it would have cost on-chain directly:
>
> {circuit page link}
>
> It's yours to share — and if you ever want more beats, anyone can post a job for your circuit and
> an independent operator runs it, watched by anyone. Questions welcome, and if something on the
> page looks wrong, tell us: that's what the challenge window is for.

## Notes for the sender

- One circuit per message, even for holders with dozens — link the circuit, not the whole list.
- The Chinese version goes first for holders whose on-chain activity or community presence is
  Chinese-speaking; both languages in one message is fine when unsure.
- If the holder's machine is one of the 7 AlephTape nets too big for a block (ran once), say "ran
  once, end to end" instead of "`{n}` beats".
- Keep replies in the same channel the holder uses; do not cross-post addresses publicly beyond
  what the census already shows on-chain.
