How it works

Optimistic execution, one gate at a time

Post a job

Name any taped-out circuit by processor and id, give it a starting state and inputs, choose how many beats to run, and attach a fee.

Claim with a bond

An operator runs it off-chain with a bit-exact TAP-02 evaluator and posts the result plus a Merkle root over every wire value, in execution order.

Watch and challenge

Anyone can re-run the job for free. A different root starts a bisection; a correct root with a wrong result is disproved directly from the root.

Check one step

The contract reads the gate from the circuit's on-chain netlist and recomputes that single NAND, LATCH or REF wire. The liar loses the bond to the challenger.

This only works because TapeOut circuits are NAND gates with exact, public semantics, stored on-chain and never changed. That makes every step of any circuit checkable by a contract, and nested REF calls and LATCH state come along for free.
